About

An engineer whose versatility comes from a broad technical skill set — and from learning quickly in multidisciplinary teams.

Background

I am an engineer with experience spanning mechanical, aerospace, and software engineering. My career began in physical systems — glazing design, jet engine thermal and lubrication analysis, external hardware design — and moved deliberately toward software: full-stack web applications, cloud migration, and data pipelines at Pratt & Whitney, followed by an M.S. in Computer Science at Johns Hopkins with a concentration in database systems and knowledge management.

That path gives me an unusual working range: I can reason about a bearing compartment seal and about the ingestion pipeline that reports on it.
