## Overview

A simulated satellite system in geosynchronous orbit: the platform tracks a
light source (standing in for sun-tracking attitude control), checks various
instrument payloads, and remotely supplies environmental readings.

## Highlights

- Combined microcomputer control with electro-optical sensing.
- Integrated payload health checks with remote telemetry-style reporting.
- An early hands-on link between aerospace systems thinking and embedded
  software. I came back to embedded work directly in my M.S. in real-time
  embedded systems.
