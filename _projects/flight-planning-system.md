## Overview

Designed and built a flight planning management system in C#, allowing a user
to manage flight routes in an air traffic control context.

## Approach

The course applied the full software development process — requirements,
design, implementation, and verification — rather than jumping straight to
code. That discipline felt slow at the time. It scales better than heroic
effort, and I kept relearning that lesson in professional work.
