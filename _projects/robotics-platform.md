## Overview

Led a team building and programming a differential-wheeled robot, culminating
in a demonstration of its capabilities: synchronized movement, environmental
awareness, path navigation, and object interaction.

## Highlights

- Coordinated team roles across mechanical, electrical, and programming tasks.
- Integrated sensing and control for autonomous navigation behaviors.
- Got my first taste of leading a multidisciplinary team — which turned out
  to be most of professional engineering in practice.
