## Overview

The Repair Dashboard is a production web application used to track engine
repairs and parts through the maintenance, repair, and overhaul process. I
implemented customer-requested changes that improved how teams track and
ensure the delivery of customer engines.

## Role

Senior Software Engineer on the Methods & Tools full-stack development team —
responsible for building, maintaining, and improving the application in
production.

## Highlights

- Implemented customer-driven feature changes end to end.
- Contributed to migrating in-production digital MRO tools to AWS.
- Produced a turnaround-time monitoring method (tracking incoming, approved,
  and outgoing tasks) that gave the operation a measurable performance signal.
